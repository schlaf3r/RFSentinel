RF War Sentinel · CSI Node Flasher

Flash the WiFi Sense CSI firmware onto an ESP32-S3 over USB, straight from your browser.

Browser not supported

This flasher needs the Web Serial API. Use desktop Chrome, Edge, or Opera. It does not work in Android or iOS browsers, or in Firefox/Safari.

Before you click

  1. Open this page in desktop Chrome or Edge. (Web Serial isn't available on phones.)
  2. Plug the ESP32-S3 into the computer with a data USB cable (not charge-only).
  3. Click Connect & Flash and pick the board's serial port in the browser prompt.
  4. If it can't connect, hold the board's BOOT button, tap RESET, release BOOT, then retry — that forces download mode.

Flashing erases the board and writes the CSI node firmware at offset 0. It takes under a minute.

After flashing

  1. Open the board's serial monitor at 115200 to see its IP, or check your router.
  2. In RF War Sentinel: long-press a WiFi access point → WiFi Sense (motion) → Add CSI node → enter the board's IP, port 3333.
  3. Set your WiFi SSID/password in the sketch before flashing, or the node can't join the network. Edit esp32-csi-node.ino and reflash if needed.

Firmware built from esp32-csi-node/ in the repo · receive-only CSI sensing · nothing is transmitted beyond normal WiFi join.
